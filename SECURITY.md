# Security Policy

The thinkNXG team takes security issues in the thinkNXG Framework seriously. To report a security issue, please contact [thinkNXG Support](https://thinknxg.com/thinkdesk).

thinkNXG Framework is built on the open-source Frappe Framework. Vulnerabilities that also affect the upstream project are coordinated with its maintainers.

We appreciate your efforts to responsibly disclose your findings. We'll endeavor to respond quickly, and will keep you updated throughout the process.
